# Third-party notices — redistribution review draft

This inventory identifies upstream material and remaining distribution obligations. It is not a completed legal notice pack and does not license Athena or its artwork.

The [source distribution evidence](docs/m42/notices/source-distribution.md) records the 2026-11-22 checks against the integration overlay and exact npm lock digest. A source archive that excludes installed dependencies still needs to preserve notices for copied upstream material. A recipient's later npm install does not resolve the missing notice or exact-build evidence recorded here.

The project owner stated on 2026-11-22 that they made the UI, and selected Apache-2.0 for Athena-owned code. This does not identify every imported or derived asset, establish permissions for third-party studies, or complete the notices below.

Retain the matching license texts and notices for source, standalone HTML and app recipients. Check the final ARM64 target graph and payload rather than using all-target lock counts to infer what ships. Until the missing grants and notices are resolved, do not distribute either the source or the app externally.
